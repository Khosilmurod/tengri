# Tengri art direction

The site presents Tengri as a platform for factory world models and robotics deployment. It opens with automotive robotics imagery, then moves through a short statement, two complementary interactive views of a complete plant, the capture/simulate/deploy workflow, and a contact footer.

The visual style uses a dark industrial palette, bright silver model surfaces, restrained technical labels, and slow motion. Navigation links directly to Explore, Platform, and Contact. The hero image and process image use separate visuals so the factory story has room to breathe.

The factory model is a fictional open 3D dataset, not a Tengri deployment. The site makes no customer, deployment, or performance claims. Contact details are in `content/tengri.ts`.
